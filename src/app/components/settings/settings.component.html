Automatic Backup

Saves a copy of these settings whenever they change, so a mistaken change or a reinstall does not mean configuring every instrument again. Nothing is written when the settings are the same as the last backup, so the copies kept are the last few different configurations rather than the last few days. Backups contain no passwords, so they are safe to leave on this machine.
